# Exact role of prior work

Boolean provenance semirings (Green, Karvounarakis and Tannen, PODS 2007, DOI 10.1145/1265530.1265535) already represent disjunctive derivations under assignments. SPLlift (Bodden et al., PLDI 2013, DOI 10.1145/2491956.2491976) lifts distributive flow functions with Boolean feature conditions; its all-configuration precision is relative to the supplied analysis graph. Section 5 of the paper identifies a feature-insensitive call graph/points-to analysis as a source of imprecision in the implementation. Our mask algebra specializes these foundations to optional clears. The additional research obligation is exact resource-indexed bad-prefix classification coupled to interruption-exposed timed schedule preservation. The retained local truth-table comparator instantiates configuration lifting; it does not execute SPLlift or establish superiority over its BDD implementation.

The manuscript's bibliography is newly assembled for this development. It is not a recovered frozen bibliography of the earlier baseline. A machine-readable source and reading-status inventory is retained in the paper directory; the standalone code needs no downloaded publication.

- **Alur and Dill (1994), DOI 10.1016/0304-3975(94)90010-8:** the clock quotient and its finite reachability foundation are established.
- **Reps, Horwitz and Sagiv (POPL 1995), DOI 10.1145/199448.199462:** exact distributive finite-fact propagation is established. The mask transfer algebra here is a specialization, not a replacement of IFDS.
- **Reps, Schwoon, Jha and Melski (SCP 2005), DOI 10.1016/j.scico.2005.02.009:** general weighted valid-path summaries already exist. The present contribution is not new expressiveness beyond weighted pushdown frameworks.
- **Reiter (1987), DOI 10.1016/0004-3702(87)90062-2:** diagnosis/hitting-set structure is established; minimum-cost selection alone is not claimed novel.
- **Wimmer and Lammich (TACAS 2018); Wimmer and von Mutius (TACAS 2020):** verified timed checking and reachability certificates provide stronger mechanized implementation assurance. This artifact is ordinary Python with separate checker source.
- **Blade (POPL 2021), DOI 10.1145/3434330:** protections, minimum cuts and real cryptographic-code evidence already exist. No performance comparison is claimed.
- **CureSpec (VMCAI 2024), DOI 10.1007/978-3-031-50521-8_14:** automatic speculative repair plus independently checkable invariants already exists. Its guarantee preserves architectural behavior while ruling out speculative behavior; it is not being criticized for violating its own preservation criterion.
- **Faghih and Bonakdarpour (TDSC 2018), DOI 10.1109/TDSC.2016.2596719:** symbolic timed recovery already preserves a subset of legitimate fault-free behavior. The authors prove soundness and termination of their zone-based algorithm; the model and admissible edits differ, precluding a general expressiveness ranking.
- **TarTar (CAV 2020), DOI 10.1007/978-3-030-53288-8_25:** timing repairs with functional/untimed-language preservation already exist. Our timed equality is obtained through much narrower edits (fact clearing only), not a general advance over TarTar's repair expressiveness.
- **Modular verification of interrupt-driven software (ASE 2017), DOI 10.1109/ASE.2017.8115634; AIM (TDSC 2024), DOI 10.1109/TDSC.2023.3339569:** interrupt-aware program verification and firmware interruption modeling precede this work. We assume rather than infer a closed environment model.
- **Hardware-software contracts (S&P 2021); Contract Shadow Logic (ASPLOS 2025); Knox/IPR (OSDI 2022); Granite (July 2026 preprint):** concrete implementation and/or relational leakage correspondence is stronger than our owned-language lowering. Granite explicitly includes precise interrupts. Merely adding the word 'interrupt' to 'contract' is not a contribution.
- **SpecBox (TDSC 2023):** concrete cache-domain isolation motivates state separation but no equivalence between its implementation and our two bits is asserted.
- **TONTOU (USENIX Security 2026):** motivates neutralization-to-use temporal obligations. No attack configuration, processor trace or benchmark from it is reproduced.

The retained research claim combines an explicit non-erasing repair language, exact joint static-plan/resource explanations with constructive canonicality, and a preemption-sensitive cleanup-order boundary. Any claim of practical deployed usefulness requires additional evidence. The paper does not claim first repair synthesis, first interruption contracts, first use of Pareto antichains, or first certified reachability.

Twelve selected TDSC primary texts have now received substantive methods/proofs/evaluation/limits comparison, recorded in the full project at `review/venue-calibration.json`. This is a targeted sample, not an established ranking of the twelve closest papers. Reading only an abstract or metadata is not recorded as primary-fulltext inspection.
